## The short answer

Could Bryan's work on Structured Agentic Software Engineering, or SASE, make him a good fit for Databricks? Yes. The fit is specific, and the strongest engineering opportunities are in New York City, where Bryan already works.

The project's correct name is Omnigent. SASE and Omnigent both provide a control layer above coding agents. They bring different agent command-line tools behind a shared interface, govern their work, and let people supervise multiple agents.

Databricks is investing in this layer. But no live software engineering posting names Omnigent. The engineering path runs through broader artificial intelligence teams and recruiter routing. The report infers team ownership from maintainers and commit authorship; it does not establish a formal hiring route.

The only posting for work directly on Omnigent is a senior developer advocate role. That means public speaking, content, and community building, plus relocation to San Francisco or Seattle. Consider it only if developer advocacy itself is appealing.

## Why the engineering fit holds

Bryan has about 7.4 years of professional software engineering experience. Python is his primary language, and he has working Rust. SASE now supports 7 provider command-line interfaces. Its scheduler, workflows, human approval gates, telemetry, and deterministic failure testing provide concrete material for interviews.

Staff Software Engineer, Agent Quality is the strongest target. This new team builds agent evaluation infrastructure. It asks for 6 or more years, strong Python, and reliable, reproducible infrastructure. Developer tools, continuous integration and delivery, testing frameworks, and observability are useful extras. Those requirements connect directly to Bryan's professional tooling work and SASE.

The major gap is evaluation. SASE has deterministic failure fixtures and verification gates, but no visible task-success evaluation harness. A small reproducible task suite, clear success criteria, and a regression gate would address that gap.

Senior Software Engineer, Backend, AI Platform is the safest level match. It asks for 5 or more years and accepts Python. It hires across teams building agent frameworks, gateways, and related platforms. Ask the recruiter about Omnigent, AI Gateway, or Agent Framework placement.

Be precise about SASE's limits. Its isolated git workspaces are not operating-system or network sandboxes. Its adoption is small. Large-scale distributed systems evidence should come from Bloomberg and Google. Lead with design decisions and failure stories, rather than lines of code.

Update the April curriculum vitae before applying. It still describes SASE as orchestrating 3 agents, while the current system supports 7.

## What to do and where to apply

Contribute in a way Omnigent's maintainers want. They stopped accepting new harnesses directly into the main repository. A Muse community harness is already being built, and Grok Build is already supported. Joining the Muse effort or fixing a core runner, host, or worktree bug is more credible than proposing another core harness.

Apply first to Staff Software Engineer, Agent Quality in New York City. Then apply to Senior Software Engineer, Backend, AI Platform, also in New York City. Calibrate Senior versus Staff early, and ask for routing toward the relevant teams.

Consider the Omnigent developer advocate role only if advocacy and relocation are wanted. Staff Security Software Engineer, Agentic Security Engineering is a stretch, but offers remote work in the United States.

If relocation is acceptable, consider Staff Backend Software Engineer, Databricks AI, in San Francisco, and Senior Software Engineer, Customer Experience Intelligence, in Mountain View or San Francisco.

Unity AI Gateway is a close product match, but requires 8 or more years and Scala or Go. Bryan has neither language on record. Mention it to the AI Platform recruiter rather than leading with it.

Infrastructure and Tools is a developer-tools fallback. The New York fullstack AI Product role is a long shot because of its experience requirements. The written report contains the posting links. Recheck them when applying: this ranking reflects the October research snapshot.
